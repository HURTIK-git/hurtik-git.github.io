Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Purpose

This configuration is used solely by its owner to manually synchronize selected files between a local computer and the owner's Google Drive account through Rclone.

Google data access

With the owner's authorization, Rclone can list, upload, update, download and delete files in Google Drive as directed by the owner. This configuration does not request or use access to Gmail.

Processing and sharing

Files transfer directly between the owner's computer and their Google Drive account. This website does not collect, host, or receive the backed-up files. The operator does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Control

The owner controls when synchronization runs and can revoke access through Google Account permissions.
